## Activity

**Activity** collects what's addressed to you: messages that mention you (or `@channel` and `@here` in your channels), and replies in threads you're part of. It's the place to catch up after time away.

## Saved

Save any message from its menu to come back to it later. **Saved** lists them.

## Reminders

Ask Sideporch to remind you, in your own time zone:

```
/remind me in 20 minutes to stretch
/remind me tomorrow to water the plants
/remind me to call Mo friday at 3pm
```

Or choose **Remind me** in any message's menu to be reminded of that message. Reminders arrive as a message from Sideporch to yourself. **Scheduled** lists the ones still waiting, where you can cancel them.

## Send later

The clock next to **Send** schedules a message for later instead of sending it now. **Scheduled** lists what's waiting to go out; change your mind there and cancel.

## Notifications

Push notifications cover direct messages, mentions and replies in your threads, and skip the conversation you're looking at. See [Phones and notifications](@/docs/using/phones.md) to turn them on. Mute noisy channels from their menu: they stay in your sidebar without notifying you.
