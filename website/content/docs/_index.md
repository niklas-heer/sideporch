Sideporch is a small team chat you run yourself: channels, threads, direct messages, polls and search, in one program on your own server. Start where you are:

{% <paths> %}
- **[Deciding whether it fits?](@/docs/get-started/what-is-sideporch.md)** Read what Sideporch is and isn't, [try the live demo](https://demo.sideporch.app), or run it on your computer [in a minute](@/docs/get-started/try-it.md).
- **[Chatting on a Sideporch server?](@/docs/using/_index.md)** Channels and threads, polls, search, reminders, notifications on your phone, and keyboard shortcuts.
- **[Running a server?](@/docs/get-started/install.md)** Install it, [put it online](@/docs/get-started/run-on-a-server.md), [invite people](@/docs/community/people/people-and-invites.md), and keep [backups](@/docs/community/server/backups.md).
- **[Connecting tools?](@/docs/integrations/_index.md)** Webhooks for monitors and CI, and [automations in Lua](@/docs/integrations/automations/_index.md) with [examples](@/docs/integrations/automations/examples.md).
- **[Curious how it works?](@/docs/under-the-hood/_index.md)** The design, with diagrams: one program, one database, and the path of a message.
